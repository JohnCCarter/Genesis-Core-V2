# Research wiki lint passes

> **Boundary note:** this page governs health-check records for `docs/research/**`.
> Lint records help humans and agents keep the wiki coherent, but they are not authority surfaces.

## Purpose

Give V2 a clean place to record structural health checks, stale-note sweeps, and contradiction
passes over the research wiki.

## Minimum lint record

A good lint record should include:

- the lint scope
- the checks run
- the findings
- the fixes applied or required
- the linked `docs/research/log.md` entry

## Good fits

- structural health checks
- contradiction sweeps
- stale-page reviews
- cross-link or registry drift checks
