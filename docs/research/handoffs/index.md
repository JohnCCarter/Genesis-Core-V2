# Session handoffs

> **Boundary note:** this page governs session baton-pass notes for `docs/research/**`.
> Handoffs preserve continuity, but they do not replace topic pages, logs, or authority surfaces.

## Purpose

Help the next human or agent understand where work stopped, what changed, what is believed right
now, and what should happen next.

## Role split

- `../log.md` captures chronology
- topic pages capture durable topic understanding
- `handoffs/**` captures short baton-pass notes between sessions

## Minimum handoff

A good handoff should include:

- why the handoff exists
- what changed
- current understanding or hypothesis
- next steps
- blockers or open questions
